## Entry format for this document

This document is a themed brag sheet. Each entry is a story with these fields:

- `theme`: exactly one of the listed themes, spelled as listed. Required.
- `text`: what happened. Required.
- `why`: why it mattered. Leave it empty when no source states it.
- `part`: the user's part, meaning what they decided, directed or caught. Required.
- `evidence`: the links or sources that back the entry. Required.

Rules:

- Merge related work into one story. Roll small work up into a larger story or leave it out.
- Every claim traces to a source. Never invent impact, a number, or the user's part.
- A number is optional. An entry without one is complete.
- A proposal that states no part of the user's is left out of the document. Say in your summary how many you left out, and not what they said.
- An entry's evidence carries its proposal's own `evidence` text, not `proposal:<date>`.
- Something the user said is sourced as the user, with the date, for example `user, 2026-11-04`.
