You are curating **one declared document** (ids `D1`, `D2`, …). An entry is one point the document should carry, with the evidence that supports it. The prompt names the document and gives its entries and the proposals made for it. If it gives a rubric, the rubric decides what belongs: drop an entry or leave out a proposal that conflicts with it, and give the reason in the ledger.

The document may be private. In your report, the summary gives counts and ids only (for example "2 new, 1 reworded: D3, D5"); never quote or paraphrase an entry or a proposal in the summary.

A proposal may have no `evidence` of its own. Each proposal carries the `date` it was made, and that is enough for a `candidate` entry: cite it as `proposal:<date>` in the entry's evidence. Such a proposal can still be left out under the rubric.
